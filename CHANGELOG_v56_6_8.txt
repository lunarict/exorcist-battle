ODERZHIMYE 56.6.8 - MATERIAL BOOKMARKS
Based on 56.6.7. Presentation-only revision.

1. Added a small embossed, aged-gold question mark to BOTH manual covers.
   No title, caption or book-within-a-book icon added to the cover.
   The mark stays attached to the existing animated cover hinge.
2. Replaced flat chapter/category controls with layered physical index markers.
   The rules book uses woven folded markers; the grimoire uses dyed leather.
   Each has a backing, rolled end, stitched selvedges, buried root and static
   contact shadow. Page-edge layers occlude the roots. Markers have slightly
   different recesses and angles; their labels are never tilted during reading
   by more than the very small material angle.
3. The selected marker pulls out slightly. A short flex at the buried attachment
   accompanies the existing page turn. Native hit targets remain stationary.
   Pointer cancellation, rapid repeat clicks, modal close and reduced motion
   are handled without queued navigation or repeated rendering loops.
4. Ten small precolored material samples; no animated filters or live masks.
   Obsolete pseudo-tab transitions disabled. Assets included in the new cache.
5. Book content, game data and mechanics, mobile ball renderer and prior card
   reveal motion were not changed. Ghost ink remains violet/lilac; page paper
   remains neutral. Original labels and accessible names are retained.

New files: bookmarks.css, bookmarks.js, assets/bookmark-*.webp.
Modified integration files: index.html, app.js (two decoration calls only),
service-worker.js. Full ZIP deployment, not an incremental patch.
