ODERZHIMYE 56.6.7 - bound pages
Base: 56.6.6. Static site; replace the COMPLETE deployed folder.

RULES COVER
Both large and small covers have no writing, plaque, book pictogram or eye.
Cloth quarter binding, rigid board, blind frame, spine bands and fore-edge
index tabs give the reference-book silhouette. Button labels/accessible names
remain outside the object. Original grimoire engraved eye is unchanged.

BOUND CHAPTERS
Rules chapters use woven index tabs along the page edge: left on desktop,
across the top on narrow screens. Entity suit tabs meet the paper edge.
The active bookmark protrudes; a layered page block and gutter distinguish
navigation from independent text panels. Ghost ink remains lilac/purple.
Neutral paper, existing editorial rule blocks, wording and data are preserved.

PAGE MOTION
An outgoing leaf rotates about the gutter, exposing the next page beneath it.
Its back has neutral paper; old ink does not change to the new suit mid-turn.
Only a clipped viewport-sized overlay is painted. The copied text is inert,
aria-hidden, has no duplicate IDs, and is removed when the animation settles.
Previous/next directions differ. Rapid selection uses the last choice, without
a delayed queue. Scroll, closing, width changes and reduced motion cancel the
transient sheet safely. The page state is committed exactly once.
The earlier letterpress/spring trait reveal is NOT replaced by peeling/flipping.
Object activation still completes before its matching dialog opens.

SCROLL AND RESOURCE LIFECYCLE
- Replaced live alpha-mask/blur passes on six trait papers with prepainted
  transparent WebP faces/contact shadows. Original six alpha silhouettes match.
- Batches text-fit style reads/writes; browser-chrome height-only resizes no
  longer trigger all typography measurements or interrupt trait animations.
- An initialized but CLOSED mobile globe ignores resize rendering. Reopening
  resumes the same renderer. Hidden/half-initialized canvases do not draw.
- Removed overlapping chapter arrival fades. The chapter transition is owned
  by one finite page-leaf animation; no continuous animation loop was added.
Globe shaders, geometry/math, roll timings, texture assets and rendering methods
are unchanged. Its visibility/resize lifecycle was intentionally modified.

DELIVERY
New: binding.css, binding.js, six assets/paper-face-N.webp and six matching
assets/paper-shadow-N.webp. Cache and versioned references updated to 56.6.7.
Do not deploy just app.js or index.html: all new assets are required.
