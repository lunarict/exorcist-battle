ODERZHIMYE 56.6.2 - FOLIO REVISION
Base: 56.6.1. Date: 2026-10-14.

GAME HEADER
The action row, player index tabs, current player, reading meter and quick tools
now form a single cloth-bound folio. The active tab joins a shared paper sheet.
The old free-floating player title and separate black status box are removed.
The six-step meter reserves hint space; long player names wrap inside the sheet.
Book and oracle shortcuts use compact physical props, not two flat panels.
The same DOM IDs, event handlers, text and game state are retained.

CHARACTERISTICS
Restored all six original torn-note PNG masks from v56.5.0. The uniform small
teeth introduced in v56.6.1 are no longer used on characteristics. Notes retain
separate contours, offsets, texture positions and modest individual creases.
Card hit areas remain rectangular. Reveal and reseal logic is unchanged.

RULEBOOK
Each chapter has one continuous paper surface. Paragraph sections no longer
have their own taped rectangles, shadows, curl decorations or rotations.
Headings, thin ink dividers and a single facts band provide hierarchy.
Chapter entry moves the whole page once, not separate scraps. Search jumps
and reduced-motion mode do not animate the target.

HOME MODE CARDS
Two separate, non-periodic silhouettes: a clipped and locally torn light sheet,
and a darker folder with a raised index shoulder. Backing layers and thickness
remain visible. No repeated all-round wavy polygon. Smaller props at narrow
widths preserve room between the home shortcuts. Existing book-cover and
oracle hover motion is retained.

IMPLEMENTATION
New scoped layer: folio.css.
Changed: index.html, fieldnotes.css, fieldnotes.js, tabletop.js, service-worker.js.
Unchanged: app.js, styles.css, mobile-ball.js, manifest.json, all original assets
and the characteristics text file. No new library, font file or external asset.
Service-worker cache and stylesheet/script resource versions: 56.6.2.

INSTALL
Replace the complete website contents with this ZIP, including folio.css.
Do not upload only index.html. Reopen the page/PWA after deployment.

See QA_REPORT_v56_6_2.txt for actual test coverage and its limits.
